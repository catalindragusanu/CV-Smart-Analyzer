JOB TITLE: Business Intelligence Analyst
COMPANY: Analytics Pro Corporation
LOCATION: Atlanta, GA

POSITION OVERVIEW:
Join our analytics team as a Business Intelligence Analyst. You'll turn data into insights that drive strategic business decisions.

REQUIRED SKILLS:
Tableau, Power BI, SQL, Data Visualization, Excel, Python, ETL, Data Analysis, Dashboard Design, SQL Server, Data Warehousing, Statistical Analysis, Reporting

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Design and develop interactive dashboards and reports
- Analyze business data to identify trends and insights
- Create data visualizations for executive stakeholders
- Write complex SQL queries for data extraction and analysis
- Develop ETL processes for data integration
- Collaborate with business units to understand requirements
- Perform ad-hoc analysis to answer business questions
- Maintain and optimize existing BI solutions
- Document data definitions and reporting processes
- Present findings and recommendations to management
- Monitor key business metrics and KPIs
- Identify opportunities for process improvement

QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, Computer Science, or related field
- 3+ years of experience in business intelligence or data analysis
- Expert proficiency in Tableau or Power BI
- Strong SQL skills and database knowledge
- Advanced Excel skills (pivot tables, formulas, VBA)
- Experience with data warehousing concepts
- Understanding of ETL processes and tools
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
- Excellent communication and presentation skills
- Ability to translate business needs into technical requirements

PREFERRED SKILLS:
- Master's degree in Business Analytics or related field
- Tableau or Power BI certification
- Python or R programming experience
- Experience with cloud data platforms (Snowflake, Redshift)
- Knowledge of statistical analysis techniques
- Experience with Agile methodologies
