JOB TITLE: Product Manager - SaaS Platform
COMPANY: CloudSoft Inc.
LOCATION: San Jose, CA

ABOUT THE POSITION:
We're seeking a Product Manager to lead our SaaS platform development. You'll work with cross-functional teams to deliver innovative features that delight customers.

REQUIRED SKILLS:
Product Management, Roadmap Planning, Agile, Scrum, User Stories, JIRA, Product Strategy, Market Research, Analytics, SQL, A/B Testing, Stakeholder Management

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap
- Gather and prioritize product requirements
- Write detailed user stories and acceptance criteria
- Work closely with engineering, design, and marketing teams
- Conduct user research and gather customer feedback
- Analyze product metrics and user behavior
- Make data-driven decisions to improve product
- Manage product backlog and sprint planning
- Present product updates to stakeholders and executives
- Monitor competitor products and market trends
- Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Manage product launches and go-to-market strategy

QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Business, or related field
- 5+ years of product management experience
- Strong experience with B2B SaaS products
- Proven track record of successful product launches
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
- Strong understanding of Agile/Scrum methodologies
- Experience with analytics tools (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
- Ability to write SQL queries for data analysis
- Outstanding communication and presentation skills
- Technical background or ability to work with engineering teams

PREFERRED:
- MBA or Master's degree
- Product management certification (CSPO, Pragmatic Institute)
- Experience with API products
- Knowledge of UX design principles
- Previous software engineering experience
